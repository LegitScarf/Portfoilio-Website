Agentic AI is the next layer of the software stack. We're moving from AI as a tool you query to AI as a system that reasons, plans, and acts — and I intend to be someone building those systems.

Multi-agent architectures — where specialised models collaborate, validate, and execute in parallel — will define the next generation of intelligent applications. Not prototypes. Production systems that people rely on.

AutoJob is my live expression of that conviction — an autonomous multi-agent application engineered to scout job openings, synthesize custom ATS-optimized resumes, and directly apply to multiple target roles at once without human intervention.

From here: deeper expertise in LLM infrastructure at scale, agentic reasoning frameworks, and the kind of systems intuition that only comes from shipping things that actually matter.
